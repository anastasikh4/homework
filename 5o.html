Professional Relationships and Problem-Solving

Homework · Business and Technical English · Level B1

Business Relationships

Maria Petrova is a project manager at a technology company in Kazakhstan. She has worked with the same team for five years, so she has several long-term colleagues. One of them is Arman, an engineer who always helps her when there is a problem with the equipment. Maria trusts him because he explains technical problems clearly and gives useful directions.

Last month, Maria met Daniel, a new business partner from a software company. They discussed a possible business relationship and the profit potential of a new project. Daniel was polite and professional, and he asked good questions. Maria thinks that he could be a valuable contact for the company in the future.

Problem-Solving

Yesterday, the team had a problem with a printer in the office. The printer did not work, and the team needed to print important documents for a client meeting. Maria called the technical support team. The engineer asked her to describe the problem clearly. Then he checked the equipment and found a simple solution.

Maria wanted to solve the problem quickly because the meeting was very important. She asked the engineer, “Is this a convenient time to talk?” He replied politely and explained that the printer needed a small repair. The team made a decision to buy new equipment because the old printer was not reliable.

Soft Skills

Maria believes that soft skills are as important as technical knowledge. Good colleagues listen carefully, ask questions, and communicate politely. They can disagree without arguing. For example, when team members have different ideas, Maria says, “I understand your opinion, but let’s compare the costs first.” This helps the team find the best solution.

The company also uses advertisements to find new clients and business partners. Maria thinks that strong professional relationships can help a company grow, improve communication, and solve problems more effectively.
